# Privacy Policy: pepper

pepper is a private tool used only by the owner of the @pepinfo07 accounts.

- What it accesses: the TikTok account's basic profile info and permission to upload posts, granted by the owner through TikTok Login.
- What it stores: the login tokens TikTok issues, kept privately so posts can be uploaded. No other personal data is collected, and no data about other TikTok users is accessed.
- Sharing: nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
- Removal: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security, Manage app permissions), after which the stored tokens stop working.

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
